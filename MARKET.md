Bitdefender: https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/consumer/scam-protection — message, link, screenshot and proactive protections.
Google: https://safety.google/safety/scams-fraud/ — Android protections and on-device scam detection.
Independent limited test: https://www.ksl.com/article/51440941/we-tested-4-popular-scam-detection-apps-heres-what-actually-worked — January 2026 comparison of Norton, Bitdefender, McAfee and Trend Micro; inconsistent results and screenshot challenges.
Hypothesis: private, explainable analysis plus recovery workflow can serve users who need practical help. Not a demonstrated market gap or superiority claim. Validate with 15 consent-based interviews, a labeled synthetic/consented corpus, precision/recall, recovery task completion and accessibility testing.
